How recognition works

Each stroke is resampled to 32 equally spaced points, centered, and scaled. A nearest-neighbor classifier compares the ordered points with labeled examples. Smaller mean distance indicates a closer match. Distance above 0.20 or a lead below 0.035 yields a review result. Direction and starting point matter. Your examples stay in this browser when storage is available.
